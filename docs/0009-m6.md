# M6: Atmosphere

Threaded as part of the wider network: stories about atproto projects show
the project itself, lexicons can be looked up by NSID, Threaded's own
schemas are published, and readers' Bluesky blocks and labelers' account
labels are honored. The design is in `0001-design.md`, sections 3.3, 9.5,
and 10.

## Done when

- A story linking a tangled.org repo shows a card for the repo: owner,
  name, description, and whether it is a fork.
- A story may link an `at://` URI. Its title opens a Threaded page for the
  record: a card for a type Threaded knows, its fields otherwise, and links
  to open it elsewhere where a URL is known.
- A story linking a standard.site document links the page the author
  declared, once the page and the record confirm each other; the same
  document submitted by its https URL is a duplicate.
- `/lexicons?q=NSID` shows the published schema for any NSID whose
  authority publishes one, with the Threaded stories that mention it.
- `_lexicon.threaded.at` names Threaded's DID, and the `at.threaded.*`
  schemas are published records that the lexicon search finds.
- When you block someone on Bluesky, or they block you, neither of you sees
  the other's stories or comments while signed in, and neither can reply to
  the other.
- An account labelled `!takedown` or `!hide` by a site labeler is hidden
  from everyone; one labelled by a labeler you added is hidden from you.
- `make check` and `make a11y` pass.

## Decisions

1. **Labels** are learned by polling: each labeler's `queryLabels` for each
   account Threaded knows, on first sight and every few hours after. A new
   label takes effect at the next poll. A labeler without `queryLabels`
   cannot be added.
2. **Network stats** are dropped: nothing depends on them, and they would
   need a full-network source for a chart. Section 17 no longer lists them.
3. **`at://` links** are open to any story. A record of a type Threaded
   knows (a tangled repo, a Bluesky post, a lexicon schema, a standard.site
   document) shows as a card; any other shows its fields.
4. **An `at://` story opens on Threaded.** A browser cannot open `at://`,
   and most records have no canonical URL: one record can be shown by many
   apps. So the title links to Threaded's page for the record, and that page
   lists the places it can be opened. No one app is the default. The
   exception is a record that declares its own page and is verified against
   it, as a standard.site document is: its title links that page.

## Project submissions and `at://` links

Section 10 names stories tagged `show` and `atproto`. No `atproto` tag
exists yet; an admin adds it in `/admin/tags`, or the seed gains it. Cards
show on any story whose link they recognize, tagged or not.

- **`at://` links.** Any story may link one (decision 3). The link parser
  accepts `at://DID/COLLECTION/RKEY` and `at://DID` beside `https`; the
  submission lexicon's `url` is already `format: uri`. Duplicate detection
  keys on the AT-URI, and a handle in the authority becomes the DID before
  it is stored. The story's domain line shows the account's verified handle.
- **The record page**, `/s/ID/record`, is where an `at://` story's title
  goes (decision 4). It shows the card or the fields, the AT-URI, and
  "Open in" links: the declared URL of a verified standard.site document,
  `bsky.app` for a Bluesky post, `tangled.org` for a repo. A record with no
  known URL has no links, and the page says so.
- **standard.site documents.** A `site.standard.document` record has
  `title`, `description`, `publishedAt`, `site` (the AT-URI of its
  `site.standard.publication`), and `path`. The page URL is the
  publication's `url` followed by the document's `path`. It counts only
  once verified, as the standard.site verification docs describe: the
  publication's domain serves `/.well-known/site.standard.publication` with
  the publication's AT-URI, and the page carries
  `<link rel="site.standard.document" href="AT-URI">`. A verified
  document's story links that URL, and its https form is a second key for
  duplicate detection, so the URL and the record cannot be submitted as two
  stories. An unverified one links the record page only.

- **tangled.org repos.** A repo's record is `sh.tangled.repo` in its owner's
  repo, keyed by the repo name. A live record (read with `listRecords` from
  a PDS, since tangled.org answered 503 when this was drafted) has
  `description`, `knot`, `repoDid`, `createdAt`, `labels`, and `source` on a
  fork. Stars are separate records, so the card does not count them. The
  lexicon itself is confirmed from tangled's repository before the task.
- **The card** is read when the story is submitted or indexed and kept on
  the story, then refreshed now and then, so a page view never waits on the
  network. A repo that cannot be read shows the plain link.
- A PDS address comes from a DID document its owner writes, and a
  publication URL from a record its owner writes, so every read from a PDS
  and every verification fetch is held to the public internet, as the
  blocks reader in the stash already does: https only, no redirects, no
  IP-literal hosts, and a resolver that drops private and local addresses.

## Lexicon search

Section 10, checked against the lexicon specification (atproto-website,
commit `88e6446`, "Lexicon Publication and Resolution"):

- The authority domain is the NSID without its last segment, reversed:
  `app.bsky.feed.post` gives `feed.bsky.app`. A DNS TXT record at
  `_lexicon.` that domain holds `did=DID`. No other name is tried when it is
  missing.
- The schema is the `com.atproto.lexicon.schema` record keyed by the full
  NSID in that DID's repo.
- DNS answers are not cached for long; the specification warns that changes
  are not announced on the network.
- `_lexicon.feed.bsky.app` answers today; `_lexicon.threaded.at` does not.
- DNS TXT lookups need `hickory-resolver`, a new dependency, through
  jacquard-identity's `dns` feature or directly. `cargo audit` covers it.
- The page shows the schema's definitions readably, links the record, and
  lists Threaded stories that mention the NSID through the existing search.

## Publishing Threaded's schemas

Section 3.3: a `_lexicon.threaded.at` TXT record naming the publishing DID,
and `goat lex publish` writing the `at.threaded.*` schemas as records. Both
are done by hand, by whoever controls the DNS and the account; the steps go
in the deploy doc.

## Labels

Section 9.5, with the research in `0008-m5.md`, by polling (decision 1):

- **Site labelers** are listed in `/admin/settings` by DID; the default is
  Bluesky's moderation service, `did:plc:ar7c4by46qjdydhdevvrndac`, at
  `https://mod.bsky.app`. **Personal labelers** are added in `/settings`.
- Each known account is asked of each labeler when Threaded first sees it,
  and again every few hours, more often for accounts active lately. The
  answer replaces what was held for that account and labeler.
- Each label's signature is checked as the research found: DRISL CBOR
  without `sig`, SHA-256, the `#atproto_label` key, one retry after
  resolving the DID document again. A bad label is dropped and logged.
- A negation removes a label; an expired one stops counting.
- An account labelled `!takedown` or `!hide` is hidden as an inactive
  account is; its author sees why, naming the labeler.
