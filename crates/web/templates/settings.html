Public upvotes

Upvotes are private. Made public, each one is also an at.threaded.vote record in your repo, and your profile lists the stories you liked. Turning this off deletes those records; copies other apps already made are outside Threaded's reach.

Tag filters

Stories with a filtered tag leave your front page, the other listings, and search. The tag's own page, and a search for tag: that tag, still show them. Only you are affected.

Saved and hidden stories

Stories you saved are on your saved list. Stories you hid are on your hidden list, where you can unhide them.

Keyboard shortcuts

With shortcuts on, j and k move to the next and previous story or comment, and o opens the story you are on. They are off unless you turn them on, so a stray key press never moves the page, and they do nothing while you type in a form.
